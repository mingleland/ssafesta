## Two decisions that were not already agreed

Both carry their rationale in the document.

**§3.4 — presigned URLs are not handed to the FE.** Deliberately unlike 007. 019's consumer is `<img src>`, so pushing expiry management to the FE means 30 images = 30 expiry clocks, and the failure surfaces as "one thumbnail in the list is broken" — hard to reproduce. 007's consumer is a single upload form: one expiry site, no bulk reads. **Not unifying is the decision**, and the reason is written down so nobody later wonders why the two pipelines differ.

**§7.1 — withdrawal delete: ordering and failure semantics.** The line used to be "metadata 행과 object 를 함께 제거한다", and "함께" does not mean "at the same time". The load-bearing clause: **the delete-queue INSERT must be in the same transaction.** Outside it, a rolled-back `users` delete still leaves the sweeper to run, and images belonging to a member who did *not* withdraw disappear — invisible in the withdrawal failure log, surfacing only when someone opens that game. Object deletion follows infra-002 §delete rules (5 clauses, 007 precedent).

## Left to the follow-ups

The server still accepts `builtin://` only. Merging the contract is not the implementation: relaxing `GameProjectValidator.isPersistableSource` happens in the same commit that adds issuance (-107).
